Google Ads promotional campaigns have age-gating, country, certification and licensing conditions. Google Publisher Restrictions, AdSense publisher account review and placement rules are separate.

Actual age-verification requirements may vary by jurisdiction; a self-reported DOB is not independent verification.
- Publish correct, reviewed Terms and Privacy statements, an operator/business contact channel, cookie/data disclosure for any ad/analytics provider, and reliable account-deletion functionality.

- No behavioral profiling, email marketing or personal-data sharing without required notice/consent. Avoid intrusive ads during betting decisions and avoid advertisements presented as casino betting actions.
- Purchase/attach a custom domain only after the owner chooses and authorizes purchase and DNS changes. Set canonical, OG and sitemap URLs to the final domain then.
